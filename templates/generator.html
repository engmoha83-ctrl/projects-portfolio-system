This catalogue is the company's, not this schedule's: it accumulates and is reused. "Applies to" names the levels a work type belongs on, so a floor trade never appears on a zone. Individual exceptions go on the applicability tab.

Applicability Click a cell to exclude that place

A blank cell means the work type does not belong on that level at all. A filled cell generates an activity; click it to exclude this one place — a roof simply has no finishing trades.

Logic rules Strongest evidence first

Derivation is automatic; endorsement is not. Agreement is the share of evidence backing the chosen relationship type, and a rule 45% of its evidence agrees on should not be read like one at 100%. A rule marked manual is never touched when new files are imported.
