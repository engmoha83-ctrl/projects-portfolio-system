Project breakdown The shape is the structure

Drag a box by its ribbed handle: move it right to put it inside the box above, left to take it out, up or down to change what runs first. A gold line shows where it will land. The chip on each box says whether it follows the one before it or runs alongside it — click to switch. Tab and Shift+Tab do the same indenting from the keyboard, and Enter adds the next box.

Work-type catalogue Shared across every project

This catalogue is the company's, not this schedule's: it accumulates and is reused. "Applies to" names the levels a work type belongs on, so a floor trade never appears on a zone. Individual exceptions go on the applicability tab.

Applicability Click a cell to exclude that place

A blank cell means the work type does not belong on that level at all. A filled cell generates an activity; click it to exclude this one place — a roof simply has no finishing trades.

Logic rules Written between work types, inherited by every activity

A relationship is written once between two work types and every activity of those types inherits it, so blockwork before plastering is stated once rather than wired across twenty-four floors by hand. Derivation is automatic; endorsement is not. Agreement is the share of evidence backing the chosen relationship type, and a rule 45% of its evidence agrees on should not be read like one at 100%. A rule marked manual is never touched when new files are imported.
